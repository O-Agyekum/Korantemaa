About

Finance runs in the family. Control is what I made of it.

Born into a family of finance, with parents and grandparents who were bankers, I grew up surrounded by market conversations and operational discipline, which shaped my early interest in financial systems.

I began my career at Ecobank Ghana through several summer internships. In 2013 and 2014, I supported digital transformation in the Cards & e-Banking Department, enrolling customers on digital platforms. In 2018, I joined the Head Office branch in cash operations, processing local and FX transfers, clearing cheques, preparing daily proofs, and ensuring all suspense accounts were zero at end of business. I later moved into client engagement, assisting around 200 customers daily and promoting digital banking solutions.

In 2019, I joined Ecobank International SA in Paris as a Forex Settlement Control Officer, first as an intern and later on contract. I reconciled 89 Nostro accounts daily and controlled FX settlements across spot, forwards, swaps, flexible forwards, money-market loans and deposits, and derivatives using Swift Alliance, Oracle Flexcube and Calypso. This experience strengthened my operational rigour and led me to pursue an MBA in Trading & Financial Markets (ESLSCA Paris), building on my MSc in International Business at Nottingham Trent University in the UK.

In 2021, I moved to Eutelsat’s Group Treasury, managing liquidity, FX and rates operations, Kyriba reconciliations, flow compliance and KYC across global entities.

Since 2022, I have worked at Danone as Treasurer / Market Operations Controller, overseeing daily treasury monitoring, bank reconciliations, FX position control and market operations across FX, commodities, interest-rate swaps, bonds and cross-currency swaps. I contribute to mark-to-market valuation, support internal controls, and lead automation initiatives with IT to digitalise treasury processes and strengthen operational efficiency.

I work fluently in English and French, and my experience spans banking operations, market processes, corporate treasury and financial risk management.
