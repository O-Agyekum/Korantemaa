About

Finance runs in the family. Control is what I made of it.

I grew up in a family of finance, surrounded by market discussions, operational discipline and a deep appreciation for how financial systems shape business decisions. I did not choose finance by accident: I chose the part of it where nothing is allowed to slip, the money after the trade.

I started in banking at Ecobank in Ghana, from summer internships in cards and e-banking (2013, 2014) to cash operations, where I processed local, FX and interbank transfers and closed every suspense account at end of day, and client engagement, where I assisted around 200 customers a day. In Paris, in the back office of Ecobank International, I reconciled 89 Nostro accounts a day and controlled FX settlements from deal booking to credit confirmation: spot, forward, swap and flexible forward, money-market loans and deposits, derivatives, on Swift Alliance, Oracle Flexcube and Calypso. That is where my rigour comes from: a suspense item is a question you answer the same day.

After a BA in Political Science and French (University of Ghana), an MSc in International Business (Nottingham Trent University) and an MBA in Trading and Financial Markets (ESLSCA Paris), I moved into corporate finance at Eutelsat’s Group Treasury in 2021: liquidity monitoring, FX and rates operations, bank-flow reconciliations in Kyriba, flow compliance and KYC procedures across global entities. That role built my expertise in international treasury operations and regulatory requirements.

Since 2022, at Danone, I work as Treasurer / Market Operations Controller: daily treasury monitoring, bank reconciliations, FX position control, market operations (FX, rates, financing, commodities), portfolio analysis with the Front Office and internal controls. I also drive automation initiatives with IT, digitalising weekly and monthly controls and streamlining the FX portfolio reconciliation between Danone Corporate Finance Services and Danone Finance International.

My work supports stronger governance, operational resilience and continuous process optimisation across Danone’s global treasury landscape. English is my native language and I work fluently in French.
