Leadership & mentoring

Making junior talent autonomous.

I have trained and onboarded interns in both corporate finance and treasury environments, at Danone and at Eutelsat.

My role covers the whole path: designing clear onboarding paths and training materials, teaching core processes such as FX controls, reconciliations, reporting and system usage, then providing day-to-day coaching, feedback and quality checks until interns become autonomous contributors within the team.

This is how I transfer knowledge, support junior talent and strengthen operational continuity within finance teams: the work keeps running when people change.

How I work with a team

  • ✓ Clear expectations and documented processes before delegation.
  • ✓ Coaching by doing: reconciliations, FX controls and reporting done together, then alone.
  • ✓ Quality checks and feedback loops that protect the team’s deliverables.
  • ✓ Cross-functional partnership with IT, controllers and auditors.
